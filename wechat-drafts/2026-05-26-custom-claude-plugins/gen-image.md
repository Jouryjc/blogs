# Shared Image System Prompt

Create Chinese technical sketchnote infographics in Deep Research Sketchnote style for a WeChat Official Account article by 蒸馏小余.

Global visual style:
- Warm cream paper background, close to #F6EEDB or #FAF4E8.
- Dark navy hand-drawn outlines, close to #0B1538.
- Pastel rounded cards, light blue, light green, light yellow, light orange, light pink.
- Clear engineering flowcharts with arrows, folders, terminal windows, docs, checklists, small friendly engineer/robot doodles.
- Mobile-readable Chinese labels, 2-8 Chinese characters per label when possible.
- Clean spacing, no dense tiny text, no fake code blocks full of unreadable glyphs.
- Editorial hand-drawn technical explainer, not photorealistic, not 3D, not corporate PPT, not cyberpunk.
- Do not use real Claude or Anthropic logos. Use text labels and abstract icons only.

Typography and layout:
- Strong Chinese title hierarchy.
- Short labels inside cards.
- Bottom takeaway strip with one clear Chinese sentence.
- Keep the main reading path left-to-right or top-to-bottom.
