# Default Image Prompt: GSD / SDD 蒸馏小余知识图解

本草稿所有微信公众号封面和正文插图使用蒸馏小余知识图解风格。

Core visual direction:

- Deep Research Sketchnote, hand-drawn technical explainer infographic.
- Chinese AI engineering / coding-agent workflow education article, optimized for mobile WeChat reading.
- Warm off-white / cream paper texture background, low-saturation blue, mint, yellow, peach, and pink sticky-note blocks.
- Deep navy hand-drawn outlines, rounded sticky-note panels, marker arrows, compact readable Chinese labels.
- Avoid real product logos. Use text labels such as "GSD", "Superpowers", "OpenSpec", "Agent", "PR" instead of official logos.
- Make every image explain one concrete workflow, comparison, decision, or failure mode.
- Keep Chinese text short and readable. No random English filler. Technical abbreviations like SDD, PR, Agent are acceptable.
- Use bottom takeaway strip with a short Chinese conclusion.

Avoid:

- Photorealistic, cyberpunk, 3D glossy render, dark sci-fi, glassmorphism dashboard.
- Corporate PPT style, generic vector SaaS hero, bright blue/orange poster background.
- Dense tiny text, fake code, fake UI screenshots, brand logos.
