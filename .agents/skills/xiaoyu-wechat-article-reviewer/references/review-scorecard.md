# 蒸馏小余公众号文章评分表

Total: 100 points.

| Dimension | Points | What Good Looks Like |
|---|---:|---|
| Positioning fit | 10 | AI Agent engineering, AI coding, engineering knowledge systems, or developer workflow. The reader immediately knows why 蒸馏小余 should cover it. |
| Title conversion | 15 | Specific pain/benefit/conflict, clear target reader, no empty “deep dive” title. Creates curiosity without misleading. |
| First-screen hook | 15 | First 200-300 Chinese chars contain concrete scene, stakes, conclusion, and reader fit. |
| Structure and density | 15 | 3-5 core judgments, sections are scannable, no source dump in the middle, article length matches type. |
| Practical takeaway | 15 | Provides a reusable object: template, checklist, prompt, table, commands, workflow, rubric, or decision tree. |
| Author judgment | 10 | Has operational judgment: what I would use, avoid, test, or watch. |
| WeChat readability | 10 | Short paragraphs, strong subheads, mobile-friendly tables, controlled bolding, images where needed. |
| Growth mechanism | 10 | Has a concrete CTA tied to an asset or next article; gives readers a reason to follow, reply, save, or share. |

## Article Type Targets

`5 分钟蒸馏`
- 1500-2500 Chinese chars.
- One concept, one update, or one framework.
- Strong “what changed / why it matters / what to do” flow.

`小余实测`
- Show actual commands, screenshots, costs, failure points, and recommendation.
- Must include “适合谁 / 不适合谁”.

`工程清单`
- Built around a checklist, template, prompt, config, evaluation rubric, or SOP.
- Should be highly saveable and easy to forward to a team chat.

`深度拆解`
- 5000-8000 Chinese chars by default.
- One thesis, multiple evidence points, strong final judgment.
- If over 10000 Chinese chars, recommend splitting into a series unless the user explicitly wants a report.
