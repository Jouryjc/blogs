# Default Image Prompt: 蒸馏小余 Sketchnote

本篇文章所有封面和正文插图使用蒸馏小余知识卡 / Deep Research Sketchnote 风格。

Core visual direction:

- Warm cream paper background, dark navy hand-drawn outlines, low-saturation pastel sticky-note blocks.
- Chinese AI engineering explainer, optimized for mobile WeChat reading.
- Use clear arrows, rounded cards, database/document/search/agent/check icons, and short Chinese labels.
- The subject is Google Agentic RAG, but do not use real Google logo or brand marks. Use text labels and abstract cloud/data icons.
- The image should explain workflow, evidence gaps, corpus routing, and sufficient context checks.
- Keep Chinese text short and readable. Prefer labels such as "用户问题", "语料描述", "规划", "改写查询", "跨库检索", "缺口反馈", "足够上下文", "最终回答".
- Use a bottom takeaway strip when useful.

Avoid:

- Photorealistic, 3D render, cyberpunk, glossy SaaS poster, dense tiny text, real brand logos.
- Bright blue/orange full poster backgrounds.
- Pure decoration without a clear engineering relationship.
