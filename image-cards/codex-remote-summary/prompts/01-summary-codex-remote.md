## Style: notion

Minimalist hand-drawn line art, intellectual aesthetic.

- Warm cream paper background (#F5F0E8), subtle paper grain.
- Black / deep charcoal hand-drawn wobble lines.
- Clean Notion-like layout with enough whitespace.
- Pastel rounded boxes: macaron blue, lavender, mint, peach, soft yellow.
- Simple line doodles: phone, laptop, server, terminal, diff, checkmark, arrows.
- Hand-drawn lettering feel, but all Chinese text must be highly readable.
- No photorealism, no glossy 3D, no cyberpunk, no real product logos.
- Do not draw Apple logo or any brand logo.

## Layout: dense

Use a structured one-page knowledge card:

1. Top title zone.
2. Large conclusion highlight.
3. Three-step workflow row.
4. Four compact insight cards.
5. Formula / takeaway block near the bottom.

Avoid placing critical content in the bottom 10% and top-right corner.
